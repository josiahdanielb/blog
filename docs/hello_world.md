The message that is important deliver is regardless of talent, youth, and wealth, 
the difference between successful people and those less fortunate is the power of
consistency. Showing up everyday, not perfect nor 100%. Just showing up at whatever 
capacity it is that you can deliver for that day, but being a person of consistency,
can an immense impact on the growth of opportunities available for you with time.
